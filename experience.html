The experience

Simple as a real sticky note.

Choose a paper note, move it into your Quest Home surroundings and continue writing right on the note.

Find your note

The dark library makes colorful notes easy to spot. Create a new one, scroll through your collection or search for the note you need.

Take it with you

Move a note out of the library into its own movable Quest Home window, alongside your chosen Home, Loft or passthrough view.

Keep writing

Edit with the Quest keyboard. Text and color save locally as you work, while the compact menu keeps color, clipboard and deletion controls close.
