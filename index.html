The experience

Simple as a real sticky note.

Built around a direct gesture, not a separate scene or a complicated placement wizard.

01

Find your note

A dark library keeps your colorful paper notes easy to see. Create another note and scroll or search the collection.

02

Take it with you

Extract the note from the library into its own movable Quest Home window. The surrounding Home, Loft or passthrough environment remains yours.

03

Keep writing

Edit directly on the note with the Quest keyboard. Text and color are saved locally; the compact menu gives you color, clipboard and deletion controls.

Product details

Made for the space you're already in.

DesktopSticker XR does not launch a proprietary virtual room. It uses Quest Home windows, so your notes belong alongside the environment you chose. It is not designed to overlay unrelated running VR applications.

Platform
Meta Quest 3; Quest Home workflow
Interaction
Home library, movable note windows, hands/controllers and virtual keyboard
Data
Local app storage; no application account, custom cloud or analytics in the current build
Engineering
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Meta VR Layout SDK; retained Meta Spatial SDK code outside the active Home flow

Development status · 2026

Working prototype, not yet on sale.

The developer has tested note extraction, editing, autosave and the child-window menu on Quest 3. Before a paid release, the signed build, entitlement behavior, broader device/recovery tests, final privacy and seller information, legal review and Meta Store acceptance still need to be completed.

No download, purchase link or public source-code repository is offered here yet.

На русском

Заметки в вашем окружении Quest.

DesktopSticker XR — приложение для бумажных стикеров в Quest Home. Стикер можно взять с главной библиотеки, вынести в отдельное перемещаемое окно и редактировать текст прямо на нём. Текст и цвет сохраняются локально. Приложение не создаёт собственную виртуальную комнату и не накладывается поверх других запущенных VR-приложений.

Проект пока находится в разработке. Продажа и публикация в Meta Store ещё не начались: предстоят проверки подписанной сборки, правовых документов и требований магазина.
