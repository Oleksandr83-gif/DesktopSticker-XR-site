The experience

Simple as a real sticky note.

Built around a direct gesture, not a separate scene or a complicated placement wizard.

Find your note

A dark library keeps your colorful paper notes easy to see. Create another note and scroll or search the collection.

Take it with you

Extract the note from the library into its own movable Quest Home window. The surrounding Home, Loft or passthrough environment remains yours.

Keep writing

Edit directly on the note with the Quest keyboard. Text and color are saved locally; the compact menu gives you color, clipboard and deletion controls.

Product details

Made for the space you're already in.

Your library and notes appear in Quest Home alongside the virtual environment or passthrough view you've chosen. Move a paper note where it's useful and keep your ideas close.

На русском

Заметки в вашем окружении Quest.

DesktopSticker XR уже готов к использованию на Quest 3. Выберите бумажный стикер в библиотеке, вынесите его в отдельное перемещаемое окно и пишите прямо на нём с помощью клавиатуры Quest. Текст и цвет автоматически сохраняются на устройстве, а компактное меню помогает менять цвет, работать с текстом и удалять заметки.
